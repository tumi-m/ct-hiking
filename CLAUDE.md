# CT Hiking App

A website for me and friends to discover Cape Town hiking trails.
Design: topographic contour lines background, colour palette white → brown → green → blue.
Features: trail maps (3D routes), Google Maps reviews, tips and suggestions.
Data sources: CapeNature, SANParks.
Repo: https://github.com/tumi-m/ct-hiking
